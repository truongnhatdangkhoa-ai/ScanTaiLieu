# Google Play listing (English)

**App name (≤ 30 chars):** Scan Tài Liệu

**Short description (≤ 80 chars):**
Turn paper into clean white PDFs. Crop, de-shadow, export. Works offline.

**Full description:**
Scan Tài Liệu turns your phone into a pocket scanner. Take a photo or pick an existing image/PDF; the app finds the sheet, straightens it, removes shadows and whitens the background — giving a clean scan with sharp, bold text, ready to print, copy or share.

FEATURES
• Camera with 4 corner dots to frame the sheet; automatic edge detection
• Fine-tune the 4 corners by hand when needed
• 5 modes: Color · Black & white (for copying) · Ink color · Handwriting (removes ruled lines) · Custom
• Several pages in one PDF; rotate, reorder, delete pages
• Export PDF (A4 or match photo), JPG images, ZIP bundles
• Share quickly to messaging apps, email, Files…
• Save documents on the device to edit later; unfinished pages are auto-saved as a draft
• Light / Dark theme follows your phone; Vietnamese and English interface

PRIVACY
Everything is processed on your phone. No account, no ads, nothing is sent anywhere. The app only asks for Camera permission so you can photograph documents.
